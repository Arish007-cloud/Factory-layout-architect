Simulation assumptions
Deterministic flow model with 0.1-minute time steps. Operations process material up to their rated capacity. Transfer lag uses route distance and transport speed; fixed waiting delays are added. Configured buffer limits block upstream flow. Equipment geometry is approximate until confirmed.
